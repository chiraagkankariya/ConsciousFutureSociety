The Conscious Future Accelerator

In a world where economic incentive is prioritized over human wellbeing, we support those who are building technology for human flourishing.

Launching Summer 2027 · Taking expressions of interest now

The Program

A Cohort
for Conscious Founders

A 16-week cohort in Boston for 4–6 early-stage founders. Launching Summer 2027.

Mentorship

Rotating 1:1 Mentorship

Private sessions with each mentor across the cohort, including researchers, founders, contemplative leaders, and more. Providing a spiritual, entrepreneurial, and scientific perspective.

Community

Our Community

Weekly group meditation, bi-weekly co-working sessions, two silent retreat days, and community dinners/bonding. These relationships will be formed to outlast the cohort, because there's strength in unity.

Demo Day

The Finale

A closing demo day in front of VCs, angels, start-up banks and other investors aligned with conscious technology, ensuring founders won't have to sacrifice purpose for profit.

Who We Select

Building at
the Frontier

Founders building within the realms of:

  • Meditation / Mindfulness-Tech
  • Consciousness & Conscious Measurement
  • Human Well-Being & Bio-Hacking

Our Ideology

Purpose
Before Profit

We prioritize those who share our vision of building a better tomorrow, meaning that purpose comes before profit. We maintain a non-zero-sum ideology — co-creation and collaboration supersedes competition.

The Conscious Future Society began with and maintains the vision of elevating the consciousness of the global population in order to foster a sustainable, ethical, and humanity-driven future.
